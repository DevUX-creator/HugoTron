# Hugo Tron world — concept idea

**Captured:** 2026-09-27\
**Status:** Homepage and rice experience implemented. Basic product and category destinations added on 2026-09-28; the wider immersive category experiences remain planned.

## Client direction

The client likes the current structure and immersive visual style. They want
visitors to feel as though they are entering a complete Hugo Tron brand world,
with all product categories connected through one premium visual experience.
Each category should remain clear and easy to explore.

Categories to include in the concept:

- Rice.
- Pistachios.
- Tea.
- Pulses / legumes.
- Commodity trading, including wheat and other grains.

This is the requested category scope for the concept, not a confirmed SKU list.

## Proposed page direction

The working idea is to adapt the current rice-led homepage into a dedicated
rice products page, retaining its interactive bowl and grains, camera movement,
product exploration and established visual language.

A new immersive 3D entrance / homepage would introduce the wider Hugo Tron
world and connect visitors to its different product categories. The rice
experience would become one part of that larger world.

The immersive approach would extend across the brand and category pages, each
with its own experience within the same Hugo Tron world.

Alongside those experiences, an additional, deliberately simple catalogue page
would present products and filters for visitors who want to find products
directly. The exploratory experience and the straightforward catalogue are two
ways into the same product range. Filter choices remain to be decided.

The current frames, corner details, frosted glass, typography, grain texture,
lighting and restrained motion provide a starting point for a coherent identity
across categories. The exact entrance scene, category interactions and page
structure remain to be explored.

The first implementation uses the supplied courtyard GLB for the homepage, with
the central white orb removed. The existing rice experience is preserved at
`/en/products/rice` and `/de/sortiment/reis`; the previous addresses redirect there.
The centre now contains the interactive Hugo Tron glass cube. The homepage category
links lead to lightweight product pages sharing the existing cards and cart, including
a grains/trading enquiry destination. Rice is part of Products and no longer a separate
top-level navigation item. The homepage has an uppercase title at the bottom left with a larger catalogue
arrow link below it, a small brand label at the top left, and the introduction
in the top-right corner. The narrower category list sits at the bottom right,
aligned with the catalogue action on the left. The temporary rice lab redirects to the
rice category; unused preview sections have been removed from the application.
