# New Homepage — Structure & Ready Copy (EN)

**Paired document:** `homepage-struktur.md` holds the German version.
Block numbering is identical in both, so `Block 6` means the same section in either file.
Build section by section against this pair.

**Note on the English copy:** shop-facing text is a faithful translation of the German.
Wholesale and Private Label are **written independently for export buyers** — a Hamburg
restaurant and a Rotterdam importer are not the same reader, and translating the German
word for word would waste the English version. Divergences are marked _(EN-specific)_.

Placeholders in `[square brackets]` are facts only the client can supply —
see `kundenfragebogen.md`.

## Translation rules for the build

1. **No English placeholder text.** A half-translated page is worse than a German-only one.
2. **Slugs are localized:** `/de/sortiment/negin-safran` ↔ `/en/range/negin-saffron`.
3. **Formatting is locale-aware:** `19,90 €` vs `€19.90`, `3,98 €/kg` vs `€3.98/kg`. Use `Intl`.
4. **German is the source of truth for shop copy.** Wholesale and private label diverge
   deliberately — keep the divergence, don't "fix" it back into a translation.
5. **Phone number is international in EN** (`+49 40 …`), national in DE (`040 …`).
