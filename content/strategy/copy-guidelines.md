# Copy guidelines

## Origin wording

Owner direction, 22 September 2026: the countries of origin are not confirmed.
Keep the grower and origin message without naming specific countries.

- Use “Direct from growers”, “Bought at origin” and their German equivalents.
- Do not add origin country names, country lists, flags, sourcing maps or country
  counts until the owner confirms the relevant origin.
- Do not infer origin from a brand, ingredient, reference image or old draft.
  Existing catalogue country references also need confirmation before reuse.
- “Hamburg → Europe” describes distribution and can stay. It does not identify
  where the ingredients were grown.
- Apply this rule to visible copy, image descriptions and metadata in both
  English and German.

The current homepage uses “Bought at origin — directly from the growers
themselves.” / “Direkt am Ursprung eingekauft — bei den Erzeugern selbst.”
