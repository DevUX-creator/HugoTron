# Homepage content review

Origin country claims are unconfirmed. Follow the current
[copy guidelines](./copy-guidelines.md) when using earlier source material.

The homepage keeps a separate purpose for each section: product selection,
buyer needs, company introduction, private-label process, ordering details,
then contact. Navigation names and product controls repeat intentionally;
headings and explanatory paragraphs are distinct.

## Changes grounded in the source documents

- **Range:** names Pardis, Aladdin, Negin and Vahdam instead of repeating the
  hero's category list. Product names come from the existing catalogue.
- **Buying options:** identifies 25 kg chickpeas as a stock line and red lentils,
  yellow split peas, kidney beans and bulgur as imports to order. Source:
  [Großhandel](../pages/grosshandel.md), existing copy.
- **Company:** introduces the Hamburg importer’s focus on oriental cooking,
  rather than repeating the hero's route from origin to customer. Source:
  [Über uns](../pages/ueber-uns.md), existing copy and company address.
- **Ordering FAQ:** covers quote information, combined delivery of smaller
  orders, transport costs and agreed payment terms. It replaces questions that
  restated the audience cards and the private-label section. Sources:
  [Großhandel](../pages/grosshandel.md), suggested enquiry fields;
  [Lieferung](../pages/lieferung.md), existing delivery and payment copy.
- **Private label:** retains the documented four-stage process and rice/pulses
  scope. Source: [Private Label](../pages/private-label.md), existing copy.
- **Shipping banner:** uses documented Europe-wide delivery and several weekly
  departures. The €29 free-shipping claim was removed because the delivery
  audit identifies conflicting statements and leaves the threshold unconfirmed.

Both German and English messages contain these changes. No unconfirmed sample
service, certifications, founding date, customer totals, minimum quantities or
fixed import lead times were added. The draft strategy's 24-hour quote promise
also remains excluded pending confirmation.
