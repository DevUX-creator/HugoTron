# Hugo Tron — client positioning, 3 October 2026

Source: the client's messages supplied by Dmitry on 3 October 2026. These clarify the
business behind the immersive home; keep the page sparse and tell the story in small parts.

## Positioning

Hugo Tron is a sourcing and supply partner: importer, supplier, distributor and B2B
partner, able to source and import in large volumes. Its work includes private label,
customer-specific sourcing, warehousing, distribution and delivery to B2B buyers in Germany.
The business serves European markets and is open to international partnerships.

The portfolio can extend beyond finished retail products to food-industry raw materials
and products requested by a customer, even when those products are not on the website.

## Product scope supplied by the client

- Different types of rice.
- Nuts and dried fruits, including pistachios, almonds, cashews, hazelnuts and walnuts.
- Spices, including ginger, cinnamon, cardamom and other varieties.
- Saffron.
- Pulses and legumes, including chickpeas, beans, kidney beans, white beans, lentils,
  red lentils, split peas and mung beans.
- Other food products and raw materials according to market and customer requirements.

This establishes sourcing scope, not current stock, prices or product variants. Keep the
existing purchasable catalogue intact; additional requirements lead to a sourcing enquiry.
No new country of origin, farm ownership, volume figure or certification is inferred.

## Applied to the home story

1. **Source:** introduce Hugo Tron as the sourcing and supply partner.
2. **Origins:** retain the established rice chapters; connect selection to customer needs.
3. **Hamburg:** explain import, warehousing, distribution and business supply in Germany.
4. **Buying:** retain home and kitchen routes; make the trade route clearer about bulk supply.
5. **Private label:** connect the pack to sourcing, packaging and importing for a customer's brand.
6. **Beyond the range:** a short invitation after the actual catalogue for other food products
   and raw materials. Its enquiry is preset to a quote request.

## Mobile treatment

From the paper arrival to the buying paths, use five composed chapters: source, land,
Indian basmati, Pakistani basmati and Hamburg. A title, short note and illustration stay
together; native vertical scroll transitions the whole composition. There is no scroll
hijacking or mandatory snapping. Desktop retains its layered illustrated sequence.

Mobile illustrations have no live colour filters, independent looping layers or Hamburg
video shader. Reduced motion and no JavaScript show the same chapters in normal document
flow. The home frame, identity, sound control and existing 3D arrival remain in place.
